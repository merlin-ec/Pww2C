## Goal
Members find people whose taste they trust, follow them, and see what they've watched and what they thought. *(prototype: "Find your kind of film people.")*

## User stories
- As a member, I want to follow people whose taste I trust.
- As a member, I want a feed of what they've rated and listed.
- As a member, I want a **taste-match** with someone once we've both rated 3 of the same titles. *(prototype)*
- As a member, I want to report a review that's abusive. *(prototype)*
